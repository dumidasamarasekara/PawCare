Principle 1 — Simplicity First
The MVP must remain small and easy to understand.

Principle 2 — Clear Domain Model
Business concepts must be represented explicitly.

Principle 3 — User Data Integrity
Invalid care records must not be accepted.

Principle 4 — Testable Behavior
Important business rules must be covered by automated tests.

Principle 5 — Separation of Concerns
UI, business logic and persistence must remain separated.

Principle 6 — Incremental Delivery
Features should be independently implementable and testable.
